About Lunar Ice Prospector

An AI-powered analysis tool for detecting water ice deposits in permanently shadowed regions of the Lunar South Pole.

How It Works

  1. Phase 1: Rule-based detection using CPR > 1.0 and Volume Scattering thresholds, filtered by Permanent Shadow Regions and terrain slope. Found 24.2 km² of ice.
  2. Phase 2: XGBoost ML model (500 trees) trained on all 7 DFSAR L-band radar bands with independent labels from NASA LRO Mini-RF S-band radar. ROC AUC: 0.76. Found 3,531 km² at P>0.5.

Datasets

  • ISRO Chandrayaan-2 DFSAR: L-band SAR, 7 decomposition parameters at 25m/px
  • NASA LRO LOLA: PSR illumination map (60m/px) + LM7/Shoemaker/Haworth DEMs (5m/px)
  • NASA LRO Mini-RF: S-band radar CPR map (237m/px) — independent validation labels

Built as a personal research project inspired by ISRO's lunar exploration program.
